Every morning, The Rallyback finds older movies, shows, songs, games and books that people are suddenly into again, and tells you why: a sequel or remake, a death, an anniversary, a story in the news. When we can't find the reason, we ask readers.

Where our data comes from

Public data only: open datasets, public charts and openly licensed sources, and every story links to its sources. We use Wikipedia pageviews in English and 12 other languages; Wikidata; Wikipedia's edit history, Current events and Top 25 Report; GDELT's news data files; news search powered by Currents News API; the YouTube API; public Bluesky posts (counts and links only); TV schedules from TVmaze (CC BY-SA); MusicBrainz and ListenBrainz; Apple charts; Steam player counts; Open Library; Project Gutenberg (via Gutendex); and images from Wikimedia Commons. We spot patterns; we don't always know the reasons. Treat every story as a lead, and tell us when we're wrong.
