About us

Privacy

The short version

We collect as little as we can. Reading The Rallyback needs no account, sets no cookies and has no ads or tracking. The only personal information we get is what you choose to send us: your email address if you subscribe to the newsletter, and whatever you write in a tip, correction or message. We don't sell or rent anyone's personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

Reading the site

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads. We don't track what you read.
  • Saved in your own browser only: the titles you follow (the ☆ Follow button) and whether you've seen the “How it works” box. This stays on your device; we never receive it. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.
  • Services that load parts of the page: the site is hosted by Cloudflare; fonts come from Google Fonts; pictures come from Wikimedia Commons; the chart library comes from Cloudflare's code library (cdnjs); the newsletter sign-up box comes from Beehiiv. Like any website, these services receive your IP address and browser details when your browser asks them for a file. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Do Not Track: because we don't track you, there's nothing for Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals to turn off. The site works the same whether or not your browser sends them.
  • No other tracking: apart from the services listed above, we don't let any other company collect information about what you do on our site or follow you across other websites.

The newsletter

If you subscribe, your email address is held by Beehiiv, which sends the newsletter for us. Beehiiv may record whether emails are opened and links clicked, which helps us see what readers find useful. Every issue has a one-click unsubscribe link. We don't sell, rent or share subscriber addresses.

Tips, corrections and messages

The “Know why? Tell us”, “Report a correction” and contact forms send what you write to a Google Form we manage (Google's privacy policy). Your name and email are optional except where you want a reply. We use them only to check the tip and answer you. If a tip becomes an editor's note, we credit it as “a reader tip”, never by name. Email to hello@therallyback.com reaches our inbox and is kept only as long as we need it.

Our data about titles

The comebacks we report are built from public, aggregate data (for example, Wikipedia page views, which count visits without saying who made them). We don't collect information about individual readers to make it.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe at any time from any issue, and ask us to see or delete anything you've sent us by writing to hello@therallyback.com. The site isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
